# PDF.js

Mozilla PDF.js / pdfjs-dist 6.3.289, Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE-pdfjs`).
Source: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js
Distribution: https://www.npmjs.com/package/pdfjs-dist

Unmodified legacy builds, CMaps, standard fonts and WASM resources are bundled
in `viewer/`. `viewer.html`, `viewer.js` and `viewer.css` are our own minimal
scroll/gesture viewer. It loads only on demand and never uses an external CDN.
